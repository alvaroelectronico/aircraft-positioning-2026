\title[Aircraft Hangar Positioning]{Aircraft Hangar Positioning: A
Mixed-Integer Programming Model and Heuristic Solving Approaches}

\author*[1]{\fnm{\'Alvaro} \sur{Garc\'ia}}
\email{alvaro.garcia@baobabsoluciones.es}

\affil*[1]{\orgname{Baobab Soluciones},
           \orgaddress{\city{Madrid}, \country{Spain}}}

\abstract{We study the \emph{aircraft hangar positioning problem} (AHPP):
given a hangar with a set of maintenance positions connected by directed
blocking arcs, a fleet of aircraft each with a known total maintenance
duration, and time-window constraints on start and finish, assign every
aircraft to exactly one position and schedule its maintenance block so as
to minimise a weighted combination of total delay, makespan, and
manoeuvring movements.  The blocking-arc structure captures physical
constraints: an aircraft in a front position obstructs entry to and exit
from a rear position, incurring costly movements whenever the occupancy
windows of the two aircraft overlap.  We present a compact mixed-integer
linear programming formulation, expressed at the aircraft level so that
each aircraft is treated as a single uninterrupted maintenance block,
together with two complementary heuristic approaches: a topology-aware
GRASP that steers heavy aircraft away from high-load positions, and a
fixed-assignment scheduler that re-optimises timing given a position
assignment.  Combining these in a safe pipeline always yields a solution
at least as good as any individual component.  Computational experiments
on 120 benchmark instances covering six hangar topologies, three slack
levels, and up to 30 aircraft show that the heuristic pipeline
consistently outperforms the exact solver within a 60-second budget as
soon as the fleet grows beyond the regime in which the exact solver is
competitive, while the exact solver closes the gap on the smallest
instances.}

\keywords{Aircraft hangar scheduling,
          Mixed-integer linear programming,
          GRASP heuristic,
          Blocking constraints,
          Maintenance planning}
