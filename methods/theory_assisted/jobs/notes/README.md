# Notes — literature digest + design sketches

Use this folder to capture what you take from the literature *before*
writing code.  Recommended structure (you can deviate):

- `literature_digest.md` — one section per paper you actually read,
  with: (a) what problem they solve, (b) what technique they use,
  (c) what specifically transfers to paper #2.
- `design.md` — your synthesis: the chosen approach, the algorithmic
  outline, the data structures, the decision points.
- `experiments_log.md` — what you tried, what worked, what didn't.
  Not the same as a `JOURNAL.md` autoresearch loop — this is a
  human-readable diary.

These notes are **private to the method**.  They may quote external
papers freely.  They must NOT contain code or descriptions copied from
`methods/manual/` or `methods/autoresearch/`.
