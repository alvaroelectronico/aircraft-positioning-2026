\abstract{TODO: rewrite for paper~\#2.  The aircraft-level formulation of
\citet{TODO-cejor-paper} models each aircraft as a single uninterrupted
maintenance block, an aggregation that is valid only when blocking
interactions depend solely on the entry and exit instants of the block.
In this paper we relax that assumption and treat blocking at the
\emph{job} level: each aircraft is a chain of jobs whose individual
start times can trigger blocking movements.  We introduce a new
mixed-integer linear programming formulation, propose heuristics
tailored to the job-level structure, and evaluate both on the same
120-instance benchmark used for the aircraft-level paper, enabling a
direct formulation-to-formulation comparison.}

\keywords{Aircraft hangar scheduling,
          Job-level mixed-integer linear programming,
          Blocking constraints,
          Maintenance planning,
          Resource-constrained project scheduling}
