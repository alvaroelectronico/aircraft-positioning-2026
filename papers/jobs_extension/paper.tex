\title[Aircraft Maintenance Hangar Positioning]{Aircraft Maintenance Hangar
Positioning under Blocking Constraints: A MILP Model and a Heuristic
Approach}

\author*[1]{\fnm{\'Alvaro} \sur{Garc\'ia}}
\email{alvaro.garcia@upm.es}

\affil*[1]{\orgname{Universidad Polit\'ecnica de Madrid},
           \orgaddress{\city{Madrid}, \country{Spain}}}

\abstract{Scheduled maintenance keeps an airline's fleet airworthy, and
much of it is carried out in hangars whose parking capacity is limited and
whose layout constrains how aircraft move in and out.  Such hangars are
blocking resources: an aircraft at a front position obstructs the access
path of the aircraft behind it, and clearing that path may require towing
the front aircraft out and back, an expensive manoeuvre.  Whether a rear
aircraft can be accessed while a front aircraft is being serviced depends
on which of the front aircraft's jobs is running: an inter-job gap admits a
cheap manoeuvre, an interruptible job admits one at the cost of a bounded
extension, and a non-interruptible job admits none.  We contribute (i) an
exact mixed-integer linear programming formulation whose partition
constraint classifies every access into one of four admissible states and
renders the impossible ones infeasible, and (ii) IGVND, an Iterated-Greedy
/ Variable-Neighbourhood-Descent metaheuristic that separates the
combinatorial assignment-and-ordering decision from the timing decision and
prices it with a fast two-regime decoder under a checker-validated safety
net.  On a controlled 370-instance benchmark spanning four blocking
topologies, four fleet sizes and three slack levels, the MILP is effective
on the small instances --- where the heuristic matches it, exactly on the
easiest cases and within a few objective units elsewhere --- while on the
larger instances, which the MILP can no longer close, the
heuristic returns substantially better feasible solutions.}

\keywords{Aircraft hangar scheduling,
          Mixed-integer linear programming,
          Blocking constraints,
          Maintenance planning,
          Resource-constrained project scheduling}
