\section{Conclusions}
\label{sec:conclusions}
% ==============================================================================

We have studied the aircraft hangar positioning problem at the job level,
where blocking interactions depend on the per-job timing of the front
aircraft rather than on a single block of occupancy.  We contributed an
exact MILP formulation whose partition constraint classifies every access
into one of four admissible states and correctly forbids accesses that
would fall inside a non-interruptible job, and a metaheuristic, IGVND, that
separates the combinatorial assignment-and-ordering decision from the
timing decision and prices the former with a two-regime decoder under an
Iterated-Greedy / VND search with a checker-validated safety net.

The experimental comparison on 120 instances delivers a clear and honest
picture.  On the tractable end of the benchmark --- small fleets,
no or loose blocking --- the MILP proves optimality and IGVND matches it,
being exact on the no-blocking and five-aircraft instances and within a
couple of percent on the remaining converged cases; the lone large relative
gap is a small-denominator artefact that vanishes in the per-component
view.  On the intractable end --- larger fleets, dense topologies, and the
profiles that activate the manoeuvre machinery --- the MILP no longer
converges within the budget, and IGVND returns markedly better feasible
solutions in the same 60\,s, by up to several tens of percent and hundreds
of objective units in absolute terms.  The two methods are therefore
complementary: the MILP certifies quality where it can, and the heuristic
extends usable solution quality to the operational scale where exact
optimisation times out.

Several directions remain open.  The MILP could be tightened with
valid inequalities or a branch-and-cut treatment of the partition
constraint to push the tractability frontier outward.  On the heuristic
side, the residual gaps that survive at scale are at the level of the
time-limited search's run-to-run variance, so the most promising lever is
variance reduction --- elite restarts or instance-adaptive restart counts
--- rather than additional neighbourhoods.  Finally, the present study
fixes a single 60\,s budget and three corner weight profiles; an anytime
profile of solution quality versus time, and a sweep over intermediate
weight combinations, would further characterise the operating envelope of
each method.
