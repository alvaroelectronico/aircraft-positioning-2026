\section{Experiment Series 05: Safe Pipeline and \texttt{fas\_2cand}}
\label{sec:exp05}

\subsection{Motivation}

Series~04 confirmed that \texttt{fas\_on\_topo} can produce worse results than
\texttt{topology\_ms6} on the same assignment (R=20: 247.86 vs 236.06) due to
movement-counting discrepancy (Section~\ref{sec:fas-modelling-diff}).  Two
improvements were implemented on 2026-05-14:

\begin{enumerate}[noitemsep]
    \item \textbf{Safe pipeline} (\texttt{safe\_pipeline}): returns
          $\min(\texttt{topology\_ms6},\, \texttt{fas\_on\_topo},\, \texttt{fas\_2cand})$,
          guaranteeing FAS never degrades the topology result.
    \item \textbf{Selective \texttt{fas\_2cand}}: extends \texttt{FixedAssignmentScheduler}
          to accept $|C_r| > 1$ position candidates per aircraft.  Candidates
          are the union of positions across $K=10$ short topology runs (1\,s each).
          To avoid combinatorial explosion, only the top 4 aircraft by
          delay$+$makespan contribution receive extra candidates; all others are
          fixed to the primary \texttt{topology\_ms6} assignment.
\end{enumerate}

\subsection{Configuration}

Shared: $w_M=0.1$, $w_D=1.0$, $w_V=10$, $t_{\text{limit}}=60$\,s,
MIPGap=0, $\delta=10$, $\alpha=0.3$, $w_{\text{topo}}=1$, seed=1.

All solutions pass RQ01--RQ08.  Log file:
\texttt{run\_experiments\_20260514\_133855.log}.

\subsection{Analysis}

\paragraph{safe\_pipeline guarantees no regression.}

For all five instances, \texttt{safe\_pipeline} matches or beats
\texttt{topology\_ms6} and \texttt{fas\_on\_topo}.  It correctly selects
\texttt{topology\_ms6} for R=10, 20, 30 (where topology dominates) and
either source where they tie (R=2, R=3).  The zero extra runtime confirms
that the selector adds negligible overhead.

\paragraph{fas\_2cand improves R=10 significantly.}

For R=10 with 4 out of 10 aircraft given extra candidates, \texttt{fas\_2cand}
achieves obj=12.35 (delay=6.09) vs topology's 16.93 (delay=10.67) — a 27\%
objective reduction in 0.2\,s.  This confirms that the primary \texttt{topology\_ms6}
assignment is suboptimal for some aircraft: reassigning just 4 aircraft reduces delay
by 4.58 units.

\paragraph{fas\_2cand is time-limited and worse for R=20/30.}

For R=20 and R=30, \texttt{fas\_2cand} uses all 60\,s and returns a worse
incumbent than \texttt{topology\_ms6} (279.29 vs 242.84 for R=20;
280.21 vs 202.92 for R=30).  The MILP is harder: with 4 binary position
choices added to an already-large scheduling problem (R=20: 20 aircraft,
multiple shared positions), the solver cannot find a good solution in 60\,s.
This is consistent with the catastrophic results seen with all-candidates
(\S~\ref{exp:exp04-fas-gap}): even 4 free aircraft can create enough branching
to degrade the incumbent quality.

\paragraph{Topology result for R=30 improved dramatically vs Series~04.}

Series~04 reported topology=225.51 for R=30; here topology achieves 202.92.
Both runs use seed=1 with 6 multi-starts of 10\,s each.  The improvement
stems from the 6th start (seed=6) finding obj=202.92, which was not found in
the Series~04 run.  This confirms stochastic variability in multi-start
topology; results depend on which seeds are lucky.

\subsection{Experiment Ordering Fix}
\label{exp:exp05-ordering}

In the run above, \texttt{safe\_pipeline} executed as GROUP~6 before
\texttt{fas\_2cand} (GROUP~7), so its warm cache did not include
\texttt{fas\_2cand}.  The \texttt{safe\_pipeline} correctly fell back to
the best of \texttt{topology\_ms6} and \texttt{fas\_on\_topo}, which happened
to be correct for R=20/30 since \texttt{fas\_2cand} was worse.  However for
R=10, the correct safe\_pipeline result would be 12.35 (from \texttt{fas\_2cand}),
not 16.93.

Fix applied after the run: \texttt{fas\_2cand} promoted to GROUP~6 and
\texttt{safe\_pipeline} demoted to GROUP~7 in \texttt{scripts/run\_experiments.py}.

\subsection{Summary}

\begin{itemize}[noitemsep]
    \item \texttt{safe\_pipeline} is robust: it never degrades the topology
          result and correctly selects the best cached source at zero cost.
    \item \texttt{fas\_2cand} with top-4 selective candidates is highly
          effective for R$\leq$10 (27\% improvement on R=10, optimal on
          R=2/3) but time-limited for R$\geq$20.
    \item For R=20/30, the primary bottleneck remains the assignment quality
          from \texttt{topology\_ms6}; FAS on a fixed assignment cannot
          overcome a suboptimal position allocation within 60\,s.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Next Steps}
\label{exp:exp05-next}

\begin{enumerate}[noitemsep]
    \item \textbf{Re-run with corrected ordering} to get \texttt{safe\_pipeline}
          including \texttt{fas\_2cand} for R=10.
    \item \textbf{tgr\_long} (Fase~5): TGR with $K=2$--3 assignments $\times$
          20\,s topology (\texttt{ls\_mode="full"}) to improve assignment
          quality for R=20/30.  Topology is the binding constraint, not FAS.
    \item \textbf{Align FAS movement counting}: use strict inequalities and
          remove $-\delta$ from $\beta^{\mathrm{in}}$ upper bound to eliminate
          ghost blocking penalties.  Should recover \texttt{fas\_on\_topo}
          performance gap on R=20.
    \item \textbf{fas\_2cand time budget}: test with 120\,s for R=20/30 to
          determine whether the gap closes with more time.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Experiment Series 06: Full Comparative Benchmark}
\label{sec:exp06}

\subsection{Motivation}

Series~06 is the first run covering all major solving methods against the
full instance library (50 instances across four scenario families and multiple
seeds per family), all under the strict-$\delta$ feasibility definition
established in Series~04.  The goal is a statistically reliable picture of
method performance before writing conclusions.

\subsection{Configuration}

Methods: \texttt{milp\_baseline}, \texttt{topology\_ms6}, \texttt{tgr\_k5},
\texttt{fas\_on\_topo}, \texttt{fas\_2cand}, \texttt{safe\_pipeline}.

Shared: $w_M=0.1$, $w_D=1.0$, $w_V=10$, $t_{\text{limit}}=60$\,s,
MIPGap=0, $\delta=10$, $\alpha=0.3$, $w_{\text{topo}}=1$, seed=1.

Log file: \texttt{run\_experiments\_20260514\_142034.log}.
Total: 300 runs, 292 ok, 8 failed (\texttt{milp\_baseline} aborted on all
8 \texttt{scn\_heavy-tight} instances, as expected for $R=30$).

\subsection{Results by Instance Family}

\subsubsection{Small instances: \texttt{scn\_few-tight} ($R=2$, 12 seeds) and \texttt{scn\_few-loose} ($R=3$, 12 seeds)}

proven optimality in $<$2\,s.  \texttt{fas\_2cand} occasionally returns a
worse solution (e.g.\ obj=32.34 on \texttt{scn\_few-tight\_seed6} vs
optimum 8.68); \texttt{safe\_pipeline} always recovers the optimum.

\subsection{Analysis}

\paragraph{safe\_pipeline is the overall best method for $R\geq 20$.}

For $R=20$, \texttt{safe\_pipeline} wins 10 of 12 instances with average
objective 212.16, vs topology's 218.12 ($-2.7\%$) and fas\_on\_topo's 234.80.
The gains come from selecting between topology and fas, whichever dominates.

For $R=30$, \texttt{safe\_pipeline} wins 6 of 8 instances with average
242.43, vs topology's 286.62 ($-15.4\%$) and tgr\_k5's 299.10.
\textbf{fas\_on\_topo is the dominant component for R=30}, winning 7 of 8
head-to-head comparisons against topology (see Table~\ref{tab:exp06-r30}).

\paragraph{fas\_on\_topo reversal at R=30.}

In Series~04 (single seed), \texttt{fas\_on\_topo} was slightly worse than
topology for $R=20$ and essentially tied for $R=30$.  Over 8 seeds, the
picture inverts: \texttt{fas\_on\_topo} beats topology in 7/8 R=30 instances
by an average margin of 44 units.  Explanation: for large instances (many
aircraft per position, tight sequencing), the Gurobi MILP within FAS explores
the scheduling space much more effectively than the greedy \texttt{\_rebuild}
in the topology heuristic, even though both receive the same position assignment.
The movement-counting conservatism documented in Section~\ref{sec:fas-modelling-diff}
exists but is outweighed by the scheduling quality gain for $R=30$.

\paragraph{MILP baseline loses all practical value for $R\geq 14$.}

For pl16 (obj 49.12 tLim) the topology heuristic finds 25.21 — nearly half the
MILP incumbent.  For pl20 (obj 199.78 tLim) topology finds 79.12, a 60\%
improvement.  The crossing point where the MILP becomes unreliable is around
pl14--pl16 (roughly 14–16 aircraft with tight constraints).

\paragraph{tgr\_k5 is inconsistent.}

\texttt{tgr\_k5} wins on 2/12 $R=20$ instances and 2/8 $R=30$ instances.
Its 10\,s topology budget per assignment is often insufficient to find a good
assignment for large dense scenarios, and its FAS step inherits whatever
assignment topology produces.  A longer topology budget (\texttt{tgr\_long})
is needed to make TGR competitive with topology\_ms6.

\paragraph{fas\_2cand only reliable for $R\leq 12$.}

For $R=2$--$3$ and pl10--pl12, \texttt{fas\_2cand} works well (finds optimum
or near-optimum quickly).  From pl14 onwards it regresses, spending 60\,s
on a MILP that cannot find a good incumbent with the extra position variables.
\texttt{safe\_pipeline} always recovers.

\subsection{Next Steps}
\label{exp:exp06-next}

\begin{enumerate}[noitemsep]
    \item \textbf{tgr\_long} (Fase~5): longer topology budget per assignment
          to close the gap on $R=20/30$.
    \item \textbf{Align FAS movement counting} with checker (strict inequalities)
          to remove ghost-blocking conservatism.  Should improve
          \texttt{fas\_on\_topo} on $R=20$.
    \item \textbf{fas\_2cand for $R=10$--$14$}: the method works well in this
          range; consider increasing \texttt{MAX\_FLEX\_AC} or the time budget
          selectively for small instances.
\end{enumerate}
